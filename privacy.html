Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

1. What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. When you upload a Helm chart or Kubernetes manifest for scanning, we store the file temporarily (encrypted at rest) for the duration of the scan. Files are automatically deleted within 24 hours of upload.

We collect standard server-access logs (IP address, timestamp, HTTP method, response code) for security and diagnostic purposes. These logs are retained for 30 days.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not read, index, or analyze the content of your uploaded manifests for any purpose other than running the security scan.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.
  • We do not store your payment card details. All payments are processed by Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified.

3. On-Premises Deployments

If you use the on-premises version of k8scan, no data is transmitted to our servers. All scan data, findings, and reports remain entirely within your own infrastructure.

4. Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or analytics cookies.

5. Data Retention

Uploaded files are deleted within 24 hours. Scan results are retained for the duration of your subscription. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing start@k8scan.com.

6. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at start@k8scan.com.
